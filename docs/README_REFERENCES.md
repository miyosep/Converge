## User-Selected Opening Reference: AgentBL

The owner subsequently selected [AgentBL's README](https://github.com/LuBryant/AgentBL#readme), specifically its opening. It leads with a short statement of the AI, user and chain roles, introduces a concrete situation, then presents a brief problem/solution explanation and an evidence map. Its award status was not checked and it is not included among the verified award examples above.

Converge applies that reading order to its own group-planning use case: product name and existing brand line, a six-friend dinner situation, the required function declaration, direct product links, a short pitch, and the supplied judging criteria mapped to evidence. The opening uses Converge's own wording and does not borrow AgentBL's financial claims or functionality.

## Editorial Decisions

- Preserve the exact required one-sentence declaration at the top, the owner's prior-work disclosure and Track A identification.
- Give reviewers a direct choice between trying the app, reading public evidence without a wallet, and inspecting implementation.
- Show a concrete result with amounts and a changed-budget outcome; link to the existing synthetic-input record.
- Use a Mermaid flow diagram to connect member confirmation, Kiln, xAPI, wallet approvals, the executor and contract enforcement.
- Move organizer logistics and the recording plan to [SUBMISSION.md](SUBMISSION.md). Link the visible submission-status section from the presentation row and keep missing refusal evidence explicit beside condition checks.
- Keep full per-flow transaction hashes, provider-call records, usage totals and evidence boundaries. Improve access to proof rather than turning limitations into completed claims.

This research supplied organizational ideas only. No external code, images or project-specific prose was copied into Converge, and no award affiliation is claimed for Converge.
