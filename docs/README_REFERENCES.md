## Editorial Decisions

- Preserve the exact required one-sentence declaration at the top, the owner's prior-work disclosure and Track A identification.
- Give reviewers a direct choice between trying the app, reading public evidence without a wallet, and inspecting implementation.
- Show a concrete result with amounts and a changed-budget outcome; link to the existing synthetic-input record.
- Use a Mermaid flow diagram to connect member confirmation, Kiln, xAPI, wallet approvals, the executor and contract enforcement.
- Move organizer logistics and the recording plan to [SUBMISSION.md](SUBMISSION.md). Keep missing video/deck/refusal artifacts visible near the top of the README.
- Keep full per-flow transaction hashes, provider-call records, usage totals and evidence boundaries. Improve access to proof rather than turning limitations into completed claims.

This research supplied organizational ideas only. No external code, images or project-specific prose was copied into Converge, and no award affiliation is claimed for Converge.
